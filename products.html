PillSheet

For older adults, people with low vision, and anyone managing many medications. Photograph a prescription bottle. PillSheet reads the label, says what it found aloud for you to confirm, and keeps one clear list ready to print as:

  • a wallet-sized medication card for emergencies and doctor appointments
  • a pill box sheet for filling a weekly organizer, marking what changed
  • a full medication list with what each one is for
Who it's for
People who manage their own medications, and the family caregivers who manage them for someone else.
Platforms
iPhone and iPad, iOS 17 or later.
Origin
Built first for my own family. Large text, spoken read-back, and plain wording come first.
Privacy
Lists live on your iPhone, not on our servers. Label photos are read, then discarded. Terms & Privacy
Status
In beta testing on Apple TestFlight now; App Store release to follow.

For people and family caregivers

Keep one list per person, for yourself or a parent. When a prescription changes, photograph the new bottle and PillSheet proposes the change. Bring the wallet card to every appointment, and refill the pill box from a sheet that shows what is different this week.

For clinics, pharmacies, and health plans

An accurate, current medication list matters at every handoff: discharge, a new specialist, an emergency. PillSheet puts one in the patient's pocket, with no portal and no account. It can be offered privately to an organization's patients or members.

Answer Reel

Trusted video answers from massive content libraries. Ask a question and Answer Reel cuts the right moments from many sources and stitches them, in real time, into one cohesive, narrated video. Linear content becomes as searchable as the web, without hallucinations: the narration comes from your own source scripts and is never invented.

Who it's for
Organizations with large training, sales, educational, or documentary video libraries that need answers, not playlists.
In
Produced video, recorded webinars, slide decks with speaker notes, audio.
Out
A 60–90 second narrated answer, as video or audio, cited to source, in any of 14 languages.
Ships as
An API, an embeddable search box for any web page, and a hosted service.
Status
Working today; now taking early-access customers.

One question. Three films. One answer.

A real, unedited run against a demo library of nine public-domain U.S. government WWII films (about seven hours). The written answer arrives in about three seconds, and the narrated video a few seconds later. It is cut from three different films, and every claim is cited to the film and timecode it came from.

For media and archive owners

Decades of documentaries can answer viewers' questions directly. Short answer videos come from footage you already own, with no new shoot. Research desks find the right minute across seasons in seconds. Every clip traces back to its master.

For sales and training teams

A rep asks a question mid-deal and gets a 60-second answer from your own training, cited to the approved module. New hires ask questions instead of sitting through hours of courses. It embeds in the portal, CRM, or LMS your team already uses.
