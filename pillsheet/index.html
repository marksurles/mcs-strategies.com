Your medication list, read aloud and organized.

For older adults, people with low vision, and anyone managing many medications. For iPhone and iPad. Now in beta on TestFlight; App Store release to follow.

PillSheet

What it does

  • Photograph a prescription bottle. PillSheet reads the label and speaks back what it found for you to confirm.
  • Keeps one list per person, for yourself or someone you care for.
  • Prints a wallet-sized medication list for emergencies and doctor appointments.
  • Prints an organized sheet for filling a weekly pill organizer, marking what changed.
  • Large text and plain wording throughout.
  • Your lists live on your iPhone, not on our servers. Label photos are read, then discarded.
